5. Deterministic Scoring Engine

Create scoring_service.py.

Convert indicator values into a 0-10 score.

Example scoring rules:

RSI < 35 → +2
RSI > 70 → -2

EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200 → +2
EMA20 < EMA50 < EMA200 → -2

MACD bullish cross → +1
MACD bearish cross → -1

ADX > 25 → +1

Volume spike → +1

Price near Bollinger lower band → +1
Price near upper band → -1

Normalize result to:

0 – 10

Return:

score
trend_direction
momentum_strength
